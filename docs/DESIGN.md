# How the house looks and speaks

Will, 2 Nov 2026: "simplify and clean it where it makes sense; improve it in both function and beauty." Learned
from Pic-Time's app (one promise a screen, the photograph doing the explaining, a row of plain verbs) without
taking its look. A draft for Will.

## Two registers, kept apart

**The rooms** are where people look: Wander, a work's page, the collection, the museum, the Keys, the reading
room, the letters, the album as the client sees it. They speak in the house's voice: the serif, the dark wall,
restraint, a sentence where a label would do. The picture is the largest thing on the screen.

**The tools** are where people work: the album desk, the Library, the vault, the stand, Getting paid, the Writer's
Desk, the darkroom, the back office. They speak plainly, like a good camera menu: one verb a button, an icon over
the word, the same verbs everywhere. A photographer sending a wedding at eleven at night must never read a
paragraph to find Send.

The client's album sits on the line: the room around the pictures, the tools in a toolbar of plain verbs.

## The verbs

Use these words for these acts, everywhere, and no others:

| Verb | Means |
|---|---|
| Share | hand a link or a file to the phone's own sheet (Messages, Mail, Instagram) |
| Send | the house writes to someone (a letter, a delivery) |
| Text | open the maker's own Messages with the link written in |
| Copy link | the address on the clipboard |
| Preview | see it as the person receiving it will |
| Download | the file to this device |
| Print | the holder's room: a print, a proof, Instax |
| Select | start choosing several |
| Add | put more in |
| Remove | take out (never deletes a work) |
| Close | end an album or a link |
| Edit | change words |
| Play | the album or a set as a film |
| Favourite (the heart) | a client's mark, seen only by them and as a count by the maker |
| Post · Story | the picture sized for a feed (1080 × 1350) or a story (1080 × 1920), credited |

House words stay in the rooms (the register, the vault, a wall, the holdings, Collect, Keep it, Wander) and are
never used as a tool's button where a plain verb exists.

## One screen, one job

- A screen has one headline that says what it is for, at most one line under it, then the work.
- On a grid, a picture carries one control at most (the heart, the tick). Everything else lives in the one
  photograph's view, reached by a tap.
- Actions sit in one row, icon over word, at the top of a tool or under a picture; never scattered through
  paragraphs.
- Explanations are a single short line in the muted colour, or behind a ⓘ; never a paragraph above a form.
- Long forms are split: the few things that must be given now, the rest editable afterwards.

## The look

- The picture is shown whole, never cropped to fill a tile on a page about that picture; tiles in a grid may crop.
- Rooms: the dark wall `#0b0a09`, the serif for titles, small capitals for kickers.
- Tools: the paper ground, the house's sans for controls, the serif only for the page's title.
- No pill buttons in blue, no generic SaaS chrome: the house's own buttons, the gold kept for the one act that
  costs money.
- Every new class prefix is checked against style.css (test/classes.js).
