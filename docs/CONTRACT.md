# The contract: notes for counsel

Source: Watermark Ventures Standard Content License Agreement Terms DRAFT (24074431)(4)(3), Oct 2021. Live text: src/contract.js, assembled per order from the rights paid for (/contract/<photograph>, /c/<ref>/contract).

## Corrected

- **2.5**: Draft reads "however you shall modify the creative expression"; the sense is "shall not". Live text says "shall not".
- **7.2**: "confirm to License in writing" → "Licensor".
- **9.3**: "any of its rights … its obligations" (of the licensee) → "your".
- **9.6**: "up on" → "upon".

## Added

- **1, 7.1**: Term: draft grants a perpetual license; Watermark sells 1-year licenses for most uses. Grant and 7.1 now state the term of the order.
- **2.1**: Permitted uses assembled from what was paid for (the nine categories of the draft). Personal uses (Share it, the Reference) and prints have their own 2.1 text.
- **2.4**: The maker's restrictions: no AI training or synthetic media; no political use; no tobacco, firearms, gambling (each only if the maker sets it).
- **2.4(e), (g), 2.5, 4**: Lifted or changed only when the licensee pays for More than one company, On merchandise, or Adapt it.
- **2.6**: Content Credentials: the licensee may not strip the embedded provenance.
- **2.7**: Exclusivity, when agreed with the maker.
- **7.4**: Draft would end the license the moment a buyer posts to Instagram (platforms take a license to host posts). Narrowed to use beyond the ordinary hosting license.

## For counsel

- **Parties**: Licensing platform named as Watermark Ventures, Inc. Confirm the entity, and whether Krafft is Licensor for the Krafft Collection.
- **9.4**: Texas law, Dallas courts. Confirm, given New York.
- **8.2**: Liability cap $1,000 is below some fees ($2,500). Consider "the fees paid under this order".
- **6**: No statement on model or property releases, and no indemnity. Getty-style tiers offer indemnity; decide what Watermark offers.
- **9.3**: Non-transferable conflicts with resale of numbered editions on the market. Needs a carve-out for Watermark-recorded transfers.
- **Acceptance**: Click-through acceptance at checkout; confirm this is enough for exclusivity and merchandise orders, or require a signature.
