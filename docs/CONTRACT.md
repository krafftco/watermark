# The contract: notes for counsel

Source: Watermark Ventures Standard Content License Agreement Terms DRAFT (24074431)(4)(3), Oct 2021. Live text: src/contract.js, assembled per order from the rights paid for (/contract/<photograph>, /c/<ref>/contract).

## Corrected

- **2.5**: Draft reads "however you shall modify the creative expression"; the sense is "shall not". Live text says "shall not".
- **7.2**: "confirm to License in writing" → "Licensor".
- **9.3**: "any of its rights … its obligations" (of the licensee) → "your".
- **9.6**: "up on" → "upon".

## Added

- **1, 7.1**: Term: draft grants a perpetual license; Watermark sells 1-year licenses for most uses. Grant and 7.1 now state the term of the order.
- **2.1**: Permitted uses assembled from what was paid for (the nine categories of the draft). Personal uses (Share it, the Reference) and prints have their own 2.1 text.
- **2.4**: The maker's restrictions: no AI training or synthetic media; no political use; no tobacco, firearms, gambling (each only if the maker sets it).
- **2.4(e), (g), 2.5, 4**: Lifted or changed only when the licensee pays for More than one company, On merchandise, or Adapt it.
- **2.6**: Content Credentials: the licensee may not strip the embedded provenance.
- **2.7**: Exclusivity, when agreed with the maker.
- **Territory** (#183; 2.7, or 2.8 after Exclusivity): only when the order names a territory (one country, named as ISO 3166-1 names it in English, or the European Union). Worldwide stays the default: an order that names none has no such clause. First draft: "You may use the Content only within the following territory: [name] (the “Territory”). No right is granted to reproduce, perform publicly, display, transmit or distribute the Content outside the Territory. The Content being accessible from outside the Territory on a website or social media account that you direct to an audience in the Territory is not, of itself, a use outside the Territory."
- **Size of Use** (#183; the next number in Section 2): only when the order states a number. Unlimited stays the default. One of: "You may reproduce the Content in no more than [n] copies in total, counting every copy, in any form, of every work or material in which the Content appears (the “Limit”)." / "…only in works or materials whose combined circulation does not exceed [n] in total (the “Limit”)." / "…only before a combined audience that does not exceed [n] people in total (the “Limit”)." Then: "The Limit applies to all of your uses of the Content under this Agreement taken together, for the whole of the Term. No use of the Content beyond the Limit is permitted without an additional license from Licensor."
- **7.4**: Draft would end the license the moment a buyer posts to Instagram (platforms take a license to host posts). Narrowed to use beyond the ordinary hosting license.

## For counsel

- **Parties**: Licensing platform named as Watermark Ventures, Inc. Confirm the entity, and whether Krafft is Licensor for the Krafft Collection.
- **9.4**: Texas law, Dallas courts. Confirm, given New York.
- **8.2**: Liability cap $1,000 is below some fees ($2,500). Consider "the fees paid under this order".
- **6**: No statement on model or property releases, and no indemnity. Getty-style tiers offer indemnity; decide what Watermark offers.
- **9.3**: Non-transferable conflicts with resale of numbered editions on the market. Needs a carve-out for Watermark-recorded transfers.
- **Territory**: Section 1 still grants use "in all media"; the clause relies on Section 2's "Notwithstanding" to narrow it. Enough, or should the grant name the territory? Is "direct to an audience in the Territory" certain enough? Is "European Union" its members at the date of the order? With Exclusivity, is exclusivity only within the Territory?
- **Size of Use**: how a copy, a circulation or an audience is counted online, who counts, what the licensee reports; whether to offer it for websites and social media at all; whether going over should cost a further fee rather than be a breach (7.2).
- **Territory, Size of Use: the asking**: a narrowed license is never sold at once. The request goes to the maker with the narrowed terms; the maker names the fee; the licensee pays by the link sent, without the click-through line of a one-tap checkout. Confirm the acceptance it needs.
- **Acceptance**: Click-through acceptance at checkout; confirm this is enough for exclusivity and merchandise orders, or require a signature.
