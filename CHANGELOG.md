# What's new at the house

Watermark is a shipped product. Each release is named and dated here, in plain words; the house shows this page at /releases.
The version is the house's own: the first number changes when the house itself changes shape, the second when a room is
added or a promise made, the third for repairs. Nothing is announced that is not already running.


## 1.11.1 · The disk kept clear · 29 October 2026
- The display copies of every past quality (q2 to q4) are swept from the disk at start and every fifteen minutes; when free space falls under 3 GB the originals cache halves and the warming waits. /healthz says what is free.

## 1.11.0 · The brief · 29 October 2026
- For brands (/brands): a brand says once what it needs (what, how many, photographs or a reel, the uses, where, how long, exclusively or not, the budget, the date), to an open call or to one artist (Commission on every maker page). No account: the brand's desk comes by letter.
- Artists answer with a fee, a line and their own work; the brand chooses; the agreement is written from the brief and fingerprinted; the brand pays once, the fee and the house's share on top, and the house holds it.
- Delivery from the vault and straight: photographs, reels and films (MP4, MOV, WebM), every file through the bag check and fingerprinted. A company's or a campaign's use waits for the releases of anyone recognisable.
- The brand looks, asks for a change or approves; approved by itself after fourteen days. On approval the licence runs, the files are theirs to take, and the artist's fee joins the statement for Friday pay.

## 1.10.0 · Hang your best · 29 October 2026

- Hang your best: your graded work sorted into what can hang now and what wants one look first. People and children are shown to you before they hang; a soft, badly exposed or too-small file, or a near twin from one burst, stays down unless you say.
- Keep back: a lock on any work that every publish respects.
- What you may do with a picture of a person, said plainly: hang it, have it collected, print it, put it in a book of your work; a company's work or a campaign waits for their release.
- Child safety: a picture read as a child unclothed or in a sexual picture is held out of every room at once and decided by a person at the house. Accounts are for adults. The kids' corner keeps no record of anyone.

## 1.9.0 · Pairs · 29 October 2026

- A master and the web copy it was exported beside are joined even when they are not the same file: a different crop, a straightened horizon, a new grade, black and white. The camera's own moment and the picture itself decide.
- When the house is sure it joins them: the older record keeps its page, number, words and licences, with the larger file as its master. When it is not sure it asks you, side by side, at Your studio → Pairs. Every join can be undone.
- Two frames of one size are never joined: a burst stays a burst.

## 1.8.0 · From where you edit · 29 October 2026

- What Lightroom, Capture One, Photo Mechanic or Leica FOTOS wrote into the file comes in with it: the title, caption, keywords, place and stars. What you type in the vault still wins.
- Watermark for Lightroom Classic: a Publish Service. Sign in by a code, publish a collection, and a re-published edit becomes the new master of the same work. Download it at /lightroom.
- Capture One: an Export Recipe into a folder, sent by the command line.
- Every master a work has ever had stays in the vault under its own name.

## 1.7.2 · Theirs, said everywhere · 29 October 2026
- Every copy the house shows carries the maker's own copyright and name: what they wrote into the master in Lightroom, or else the house's record of them.
- A picture whose camera signed it at capture says so on its page: signed by Leica (or any camera maker) at the moment it was made, with the master kept unbroken. That signature is found even when it sits under a Lightroom export.

## 1.7.1 · The maker's colour · 29 October 2026
- Every copy the house shows keeps the colour the maker made: a master in a wide colour space is shown in Display P3, so recent iPhones, iPads and Macs see the reds and greens that used to be clipped. sRGB stays sRGB.
- A work's thumbnail and display copies are made the moment it arrives, kept-back work included, so the Library shows a 60 MP master at once.
- No GPS or serial numbers ride on a copy for the walls.
- The house's standard for showing work: docs/PICTURES.md.

## 1.7.0 · A museum for everyone · 29 October 2026
- Anyone who makes work can open their own museum in a minute: put the work in, keep all of it, hang the best. You are its curator, with 36 works on view at a time and the rest in storage, deeded as yours.
- Watermark's own halls hold what has earned a place: work that someone collected, a curator hung or two people called Bravo, and every work by a maker the house has invited.
- Follow anyone: an artist, a collector or a curator. Who you follow, and who follows you, is private; a page shows its collectors.
- Following, in Wander: what the people you follow did lately. New work on view, a wall hung, a piece collected.
- A curator's share of what sells through their walls is now 10%, from the house's part.

## 1.6.0 · Send from your computer · 29 October 2026
- A whole archive goes in from your own computer: one command, a folder or a drive, overnight. It reads the stars you gave each picture in Lightroom and sends only what clears your line, and it can take each work's place from its folder (08_FLORENCE becomes Florence).
- You let a computer in with a code, approved where you are already signed in. It can put work into your vault and nothing else, and you can end it from Your computers.
- Every file is fingerprinted, the house is asked before each one, and nothing it already holds is sent. Stop it any time, and the same line carries on, part by part.

## 1.5.2 · The proof · 29 October 2026
- Every file is fingerprinted on your computer before it leaves, and kept only when the bytes that land are the same, byte for byte: Verified, beside each one.
- An import is one thing: its own page, file by file, open on any device, a receipt to keep, and a note when it is done.
- What the house already holds is never sent again; files go three at a time.
- Two pictures are called the same only when they look alike pixel for pixel.
- A fire drill every night: a real import with everything a real day throws at it, checked to the file.

## 1.5.1 · A city's worth · 29 October 2026
- The vault takes hundreds of full-size masters at once: the pictures beside each file are drawn small and one at a time, so choosing a whole shoot no longer stops the browser.
- Put in the vault once: Send cannot be pressed twice, and the same file is never sent twice.
- The next frame of a scene comes in as its own work; only a clearly larger file takes a master's place.

## 1.5 · The standard · 28 October 2026
- The books (Studio → House → The books): the month's money events one a line with the Stripe id, the house's part and whose money the rest is; the month by revenue class; what the house holds for others at month end; and the one-pager for the bookkeeper. For Zeni, the finance back office of Krafftco LLC.
- The house, anywhere: `docker compose up` runs the web, the worker, Postgres and an S3-compatible vault from one file (docs/ANYWHERE.md). The owner's view on the desk (/owner at fullerton.cloud): the house by its own instruments.
- The peephole: a brief room for one reader, opened by letter from Studio → People → The peephole: the brief (what this is, why it matters, the record so far, Studios, the first company, the platform in prospect, the archive and the museum, the ask) with the house's live numbers in it, the numbers as they stand, the construction site, lately on the register, the papers, and a line for the director. Private; nothing on the public register.
- The open verifier (open/watermark-verify, MIT, no dependencies): the papers in a file, the register, an anchor, an archival master, checked without asking the house. /platforms, the door as a person reads it. Build on it in the footer.
- The register is anchored: every hour its head is committed into Bitcoin through OpenTimestamps (free, no token); the proofs stand beside the register (/ledger/anchors.json, one .ots each) and the register page says to which entry and block it stands. `ots verify` checks it against a chain the house does not run.
- /standard: the six parts of a Watermark work (the record, the number, the register, the anchor, the file, the mark, the identity) and how anyone checks each with free tools.
- The platform door (/api/partner/v1): a platform opens a creator's studio, puts a work in by address, reads it back by its own id, resolves any picture to its record, and sells from its own surface with the badge (/w/:slug/badge.svg); its share from the house's side, the terms published and the same for everyone.
- For an audience: Wander's open feed draws from a pool kept a minute, the shelf is read once in five, one record a work in Wander, the API's list once a minute; the load workflow's key.

## 1.4 · The worker · 28 October 2026
- The live house moved onto Postgres (docs/MOVE.md), with the register's head the same before and after.
- `ROLE` (web | worker | all) splits the house into a process that serves people and one that runs its chores, from one image; the worker's heartbeat on /healthz and in the watch (docs/WORKER.md). Nothing changes until it is set.
- Closed for the moment: a read-only switch for a move or a restore (Studio → Launch).
- Backups run off the event loop; the Studio door answers again (the bag check's desk had shadowed it); founders.js makes the settings table it reads at mount.

## 1.3 · Two engines · 27 October 2026
- The house runs on Postgres when DATABASE_URL names a server, SQLite otherwise, with no room rewritten: the bridge (a worker thread the main thread waits on) and the dialect (SQLite's idiom into Postgres's, by rule), proven by every statement in the source prepared against a real Postgres and the whole suite passing on both.
- Backups and the restore drill on Postgres (pg_dump, a scratch-database restore, the register verified); `scripts/to-postgres.js` moves a house and proves the move; CI runs the suite on both engines.
- Launch checks the Stripe webhook for all six events the house listens for.

## 1.2.1 · The morning digest · 27 October 2026

One letter a day to the house: the week in numbers, what went wrong, how fast, the state of the keeping (vault, backups, fixity, conservation, mail), what needs the house. And an alarm, at most once an hour, only when something is wrong.

## 1.2 · Your devices · 27 October 2026

Every phone and computer signed in as you, on one page (Yours → Settings → Your devices). The house writes to you when a sign-in comes from a device it has not seen; you can sign one out, or everywhere at once, and only a fresh code to your email gets back in. A letter when a bank is connected, and when another address is given the keys to a studio.

- The house boots quietly: its own chores wait three minutes and take turns in a lane of their own; a book's pictures or a feed pressed by hand never wait behind them.
- Two AWS accounts, two scoped keys; no root keys; the cold page lives a minute, not a day.
- Carnegie whole: numbered chapters joined to their titles, the apparatus dropped, the binding's wave drawn complete.

## 1.0 · The house opens · 27 October 2026

The first release. Built in twelve days (16–27 October 2026), 500 commits, and now run as a product: a development
room, a gate, a watch, a walk after every deploy, and the showing switch.

- **Wander.** The house's front door and its app: one work a screen, whole and true; two fingers to look closer; the
  shelf (a passage, a book, a record) among the pictures; the Keys and Yours a swipe to either side; it keeps going
  without a signal.
- **Watermark.** Under every picture the record: the number, the deed, the register, the Shield, the licence to use it.
  Collect for a few dollars; the maker is paid every Friday; the house's share is on top and published.
- **Read.** The reading room and the Library: 245 books of the public domain in the finest editions, bound by the house,
  with their pictures, facing translations, a commonplace book and a reading list.
- **Listen.** The Listening Room: the shellac and the open repertoire, a turntable that plays through the whole house.
- **Collect.** A collector's house: the gallery, the holder's room, prints and the proof, the cabinet, the papers, a
  tour in your own voice, guests of the house.
- **Make.** The vault, the Library module, the darkroom, the Writer's Desk, Draw, the album, releases signed on the
  spot, the clearance desk, the artists behind the work, the editions room, the press and the lab.
- **For museums.** One door for rights; a collection in from IIIF, the Met's API, OAI-PMH, CSV, Smithsonian Open
  Access, Wikimedia Commons, the Library of Congress, the Internet Archive, Standard Ebooks; audio guides as objects;
  a museum's own house.
- **The Keys.** The members' club: the benches, the balcony, the season, the Club, the Screening Room, the rooms by
  the clock, the host's chair.
- **The house itself.** The register, nightly sealed backups with a restore drill, the restoration room, the bag check,
  the Studio door, the audit, the governor, the edge.
