# Watermark v0: handoff for any Claude session working here

Watermark is a Krafft company: hold, show, license and protect photographs at the source. Krafft Studios' collection (Will Fullerton) is the first maker. This folder is the working app, built in a cloud session on 16 Oct 2026; the git history lives there (commits up to c9fc719). Files here are synced copies.

## Run it
- `npm install`, then `ADMIN_PASSWORD=... npm start` → http://localhost:3000 (Studio at /studio).
- `npm test` → end-to-end smoke test; must print "all flows pass".
- `node scripts/seed.js <path to TRIP_OUT_EAST_EXPORTS/EUROPE>` loads the first ten originals.

## Map
- `src/app.js` routes; `src/views.js` public pages; `src/studio-views.js` Studio; `src/rules.js` the maker's per-work terms; `src/seal.js` sealed .wmk format; `src/provenance.js` EXIF stamp + PDF certificate; `src/ledger.js` hash-chained ledger; `src/mail.js` Resend email; `public/seal-viewer.js` browser viewer for .wmk.
- Flows: one-tap license (/license/:slug → /buy → Stripe Checkout → webhook/thanks grants, emails file + certificate, maker gets "Sold"); ask-the-maker requests; renewals and expiry; sightings; maker applications; public life page /w/:slug/life; sealed files (Studio seals → /k/:id key desk → /view).
- Design record for the sealed format: `docs/SEALED.md`.
- Krafft integration (one place, served by Watermark): `public/house.js` (house bar on every site; also joins krafftco.com/license and /watermark to Watermark) and `public/k.js` (the K seal on every Krafft Studios photograph, loaded in Ghost's Code injection; matched via `/api/k` and `/api/match` from `studios_imports`). Patronage: `src/patronage.js` (/m/:slug). Series: `src/series.js`.
- The Watermark Shield: `src/credentials.js` reads Content Credentials (C2PA) on arrival and signs every delivered file (maker, copyright holder, license, no AI training; original kept as parent ingredient). Signing needs C2PA_CERT + C2PA_KEY in Render (a certificate from a CA on the C2PA trust list); without them files go out unsigned. Shield panel: `shieldBlock` in views.js (work page, life page); shield drawn on the license PDF. Test: `test/credentials.js`.
- Tips: `src/tips.js` (/tip/:post). Every Studios story carries credits (words from Ghost's author, photographs from Watermark; narration, film, music can be added in the `credits` table); a tip is shared evenly or sent to one person. Writers are maker records of kind 'writer'. The K's end band on each story shows the credits and a Tip button. Money lands in Krafft's Stripe and is recorded per person; direct payouts need Stripe Connect.
- The market: `src/market.js` (/market, /market/:id, /studio/market). Collectors offer numbered limited-edition pieces from Yours; buyers ask to buy (no charge); the Studio settles each trade by hand and records it (price, 10% royalty to the maker, 5% fee, drafts), and the buyer takes the piece through a claim link. Work price = last trade, else issue price; /yours shows the collection's value. Automatic settlement needs counsel + Stripe Connect; the license terms say non-transferable and need revising with counsel.
- Reference: a photograph at 40 MP or more (Leica Q3 60, Hasselblad X2D 100) carries the Reference badge (`refTag` in views.js; on plates and the work page) and can be bought as 'Collect the Reference' (use key `reference`, draft $25), which delivers the full original. Ordinary Collect delivers the 2400px version. Studio → Editions proposes edition sizes for approval.
- U.S. Copyright Office bridge: `src/copyright.js` (/studio/copyright). No public filing API exists, so Watermark groups works the Office's way (GRPPH: up to 750 by one author published in one year; GRUPH unpublished; $55 each), exports the title list CSV and deposit ZIP, tracks filed (service request) and registered (VA number), and writes the number onto every work in the group: 'U.S. Registered' badge (`regTag`) on plates, the work page and the Shield.
- Look closer: `src/closer.js` (/closer/:slug; `closerBlock` in views.js; `public/closer.js` reads it aloud with the browser's voice). A guide written by Claude from each held photograph and its record, filled in the background (needs ANTHROPIC_API_KEY; model CLOSER_MODEL, default claude-sonnet-5-5). Always labelled as AI; never identifies people. The maker can correct and approve (labelled approved), hide, or rewrite. Test: `test/closer.js`.
- Titles are set in title case (`src/titles.js`) on arrival and once over what is held; the maker's wording is kept in `works.title_given`. Slugs don't change.
- The contract: `src/contract.js`. Counsel's 2021 Standard Content License Agreement, assembled per order from the rights paid for (the nine permitted uses of clause 2.1; Adapt it, More than one company, On merchandise, Exclusively; the maker's never list as restrictions). Each use carries a bundle; added uses +$150, extended rights priced or asked of the maker (drafts). Buyer chooses on /license (`rightsPanel`, `public/rights.js`), reads it live (/contract/:slug), keeps it (/c/:ref/contract). Stored on `licenses.rights`. Review notes for counsel: `docs/CONTRACT.md` and /studio/contract. Old pattern came from ARCHIVE_DEV/watermark-prod license-sections.
- Market settlement: `src/market.js`. With MARKET_SETTLEMENT=stripe (after counsel, with Connect on): Buy it now / pay an accepted offer via Checkout; the piece moves at once; `payouts` (collector share, photographer royalty; the house keeps its own) paid by Connect transfers once the owner adds a bank (/yours/payouts); a second payment for a sold piece is refunded. Webhook events used: checkout.session.completed, checkout.session.expired, account.updated. Off = brokered by hand as before.
- The license page (/c/:ref, `certificate` in views.js): a receipt in plain words: what you can and can't do (from the contract), term bar, certificate of authenticity with the maker's signature, acceptance at payment, Licensing simply. Hashes only under "How we know it's genuine". Never say blockchain; the ledger is called the register in public copy (URL /ledger stays). Checkout states acceptance of the agreement; the accepted text's fingerprint is stored in licenses.rights.hash.
- Quality control: MIN_MP in app.js (share 1, story and company 12, audience and wall 24, reference 40; drafts). A use is offered only when the held file meets it (`offered`), on Watermark and on the K. Studio → Masters lists works waiting and takes a master per work (/studio/works/:slug/master: same picture by dhash, larger), via `replaceMaster`.
- Owners: `heldBy(req, w)`; the work page shows In your collection (download, license, put it up / withdraw) and leaves held uses out of the menu; /buy and one-tap refuse a second sale of a held use.
- Seats: `src/seats.js`. Each license carries seats (share 1, story 3, company 5, audience 10; drafts; extra seats $50 each via Checkout, `extra_seats` on the license): the holder adds people on the license page (/c/:ref, owner), each gets /seat/:token to keep it in their collection and take the file (delivered by `deliver`, never the holder's key); every seat recorded. Seats stay inside the licensee; another company is a new license.
- Downloads: counted per license (DL in app.js: collect 5, story 10, company 25, audience 50, print 5; drafts; `dl_extra` on the license), shared with seats, recorded in `downloads`; 410 when used up. Every license includes the share-size copy (/d/:token?size=share) and the right to share; holding any license covers Collect (`covers`).
- The ladder (`ladder` in app.js, LADDER = [1, 1.5, 2, 3]; drafts): a limited edition's Collect price steps up by quarter as it sells; the work page shows it; the market's issue price (last numbered sale) follows, so holders' values rise. Cards show value vs paid.
- The $5 piece is called Collect everywhere (use key stays `social`).
- Applause and notes: `src/applause.js`. One tip of the hat per person per work (toggle; POST /w/:slug/applaud, JSON for the page, `public/applause.js`), shown only on the work ("N tips of the hat"); Studio → Works sorts by Most applauded and Seen, never applauded (the duds, 20+ views). The guest book (table `notes`, section id #notes, styled .gb as a bound book): a line and a name (both required), no links, the plain insults turned away (UNKIND), 3 per 10 min; the maker, the Studio or the writer can remove. The signature opens the writer's collection when public. Regard: the maker marks a line 'well seen' (`seen_at`; POST /w/:slug/notes/:id/seen); a writer's lines and regard show on their collection page (In the guest books; Tips of the hat). No down-votes, no public rating of reviewers: the makers' regard is the only rating. The epigraph above the book (.nt-ep) is original house copy, a draft.
- Studios for everyone: /join (name + email) opens a maker account at once (signed in, `joined_at`, linked to the buyer cookie via `makers.buyer_id`); /account/profile (name, house, place, bio, site, instagram, portrait → `portraits/` in DATA_DIR, served at /portrait/:handle) shows on /m/:slug. `makers.standing` open|held: the Studio holds a maker from Accounts (their work goes private, publishing blocked) and releases them.
- Friends of the house: `src/friends.js` (/friends): Friend $10, Patron $50, Benefactor $250 a month, or any amount once (drafts), to Watermark or Krafft; wall of friends; the thanks page lets a friend set their name. Keeping (same table, kind 'keeping'): a maker's archive is free to 200 works or 5 GB (drafts), then $15/month via /account/keeping; a notice, never a block, until Will confirms. `works.bytes` records each original's size. Not tax-deductible; a foundation is the plan, said on the page.
- The maker page states the split: "85% of every sale is <first name>'s".
- Scope: photographs first. Music and film are earmarked, not built: the schema already keys works by maker and kind; add kinds when the time comes.
- The house guest book: /guestbook (`guestbook` in views.js; routes in applause.js). One book for all of Krafft in chapters (`notes.chapter`: krafft, studios, advisory, ventures, watermark, dgc; lines on photographs have work_id and count as the Watermark chapter; house lines have work_id=0). Signed from any site through the house bar's 'Guest book' link (house.js), which carries the chapter and a way back. Footer link on Watermark.
- Stars and the cull: `works.stars` 0–5 (the maker's grade; 5 sets `curated`, the jewel). Graded on the maker's studio (click or keys 1–5, 0 clears; `public/stars.js`; POST /account/works/:slug/stars), filtered by ?stars=N. POST /account/cull {from}: public works below N stars go private (never deleted; still fingerprinted and registered), published works at or above N come back. Culled work weighs a quarter toward Keeping's free line. Studio → Works sorts 'The rubies: by stars'. The standard is the Frick's: the best work in front, the rest kept.
- The guard (`public/guard.js`, on the work page via window.__WM_GUARD): a near-invisible trace lattice (accession · viewer code · watermark) over every protected image, so screenshots carry it; a quiet mark (wave + address) in the corner; when the page loses focus or Print Screen is pressed the watermark comes up at once (big wave, lattice at .35) and a nudge offers Collect. Print Screen and copy are recorded in `captures` (POST /w/:slug/capture) and shown on Studio → Works. The Mac's capture keys cannot be seen by a page; the honest line is trace + mark, not detection.
- Your collection shows pieces as cards (`card` in views.js; .tc in style.css).

## Rules (from Will)
- Restrained, precise voice; no hype. Anything written in Will's voice is a draft until he confirms.
- Draft prices: In a story $350, For a company $750, Before an audience $2,500, On a wall $250. "85% to the maker" on the home page is a draft.
- Never paste secrets (Stripe keys, passwords) into chat or code; they go in the host's environment settings.

- Deleting files in Will's folders isn't permitted from the cloud session; leftovers go to `_to_delete`.
