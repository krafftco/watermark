# first-ten (The House)
**Purpose.** A photographer's first ten minutes: join, bring work in, hang it, see their museum, share it, with nothing confusing on a phone.
**When.** One `lane:onboarding` issue at a time; and a fresh walk after any change to the join, vault, desk or work pages.
**Do.** Run `scripts/first-ten.js`; read its screenshots as a stranger would. Count words and presses; fewer is better. Words follow issue #22: "your museum" is the public page, "your studio" the desk, "kept back" the state.
**Leave.** A pull request to `next`, or issues with the screenshot attached in words (step, what was seen, what was expected).
**Never.** Add a room or a step; change Will's words without marking the change DRAFT in the pull request.
