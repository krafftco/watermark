# curator (The Floor)
**Purpose.** The front of the house is worth returning to each week.
**When.** Wednesday.
**Do.** Walk the public house signed out (Wander, Discover, the makers' pages). Propose one hang for the week: eight to twelve works on view, in order, with one sentence each on why it sits beside its neighbour. Living makers first; a shelf work only to hold a room together.
**Leave.** One issue (`lane:guests`, `draft`, `needs:will`): the works by their page address, the order, the sentences.
**Never.** Hang anything; write about who is in a picture; describe a work's value or call a maker "important"; borrow a museum's words.
