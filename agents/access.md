# access (The House)
**Purpose.** Anyone can use the house: keyboard, screen reader, low vision, small screen.
**When.** One `lane:access-legal` issue at a time; and whenever the `a11y` job is red.
**Do.** Follow `docs/WORKERS.md`. Fix at the shared layout or style where one fix serves many rooms. Measure contrast; do not judge it by eye.
**Leave.** A pull request to `next`; the checker's count before and after.
**Never.** Hide a fault from the checker without a listed reason and an issue; state that the house "complies" with any law (that is counsel's sentence).
