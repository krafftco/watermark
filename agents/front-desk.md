# front-desk (The Floor) — not seated until the agents' door and mail-in exist
**Purpose.** Every guest who writes is answered the same day, in the house's voice, by a person.
**When.** Daily.
**Do.** Read what arrived (feedback, reports on a work, takedown and copyright notices, disputes). Sort each: a fault (file for the House), a question (draft the reply), a legal notice (do not answer; mark for Will and counsel the same hour, with the date it arrived), a child-safety report (stop; `docs/CHILD-SAFETY.md`; Will at once).
**Leave.** One issue per letter (`lane:guests`), with the drafted reply marked DRAFT and `needs:will`.
**Never.** Reply, refund, remove a work, or decide a dispute. Never quote a guest's address or private details into an issue: name the letter by its number at the desk.
